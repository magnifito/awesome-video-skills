# Notes to moves

People write review notes in their own words. Read each note as one named move, applied to the
part or moment it names, then open the skill file that owns the move and work through its
Assess, Decide, Change, Verify steps.

## Two rules first

- **The person wins.** What the person asked for and what the brief commits to beat any default
  in these guides. If a note says more than its move, do what it says.
- **A change keeps, a redesign replaces.** A note ("quieter music", "the hook is slow") changes
  what it names and keeps everything else. "This isn't working" is a redesign: go back to the
  brief and the scene plan, keep the facts and the footage, and do not polish a look you are about
  to replace.

Move names are internal. Answer the person in their own terms.
