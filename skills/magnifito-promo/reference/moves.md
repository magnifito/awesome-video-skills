# Moves: quieter, bolder, delight

How a review note maps to a named move: [`../../magnifito-video/reference/moves.md`](../../magnifito-video/reference/moves.md).
Change only what the note names. Compare the same frames before and after
(`mgvideo thumbnail <final> -t <s>`, or the sheets in
[`../../magnifito-deliver/reference/render-qa.md`](../../magnifito-deliver/reference/render-qa.md)).

## quieter

"Too much", "tone it down", "the glows", "too busy". Less of what the note names; the piece keeps
its point of view.

- **Assess.** Find what pulls the eye or ear more than its meaning earns: light, colour, motion,
  sound, a layer. Loudness usually comes from more than one place.
- **Decide.** The one accent that stays loud. Remove, or turn down: removing a layer is cleaner than
  lowering everything a little.
- **Change.** Take away first (the second glow, the extra layer, the repeated whoosh), then reduce
  what is left on the named part only. Motion: fewer things moving at once, smaller moves, no
  overshoot (a `calm` tempo in `brand.json`, or a recipe without overshoot). Colour: lower saturation
  around the accent, not on it. Sound: see remix in `magnifito-audio`. Never use blur, fog or grain
  to soften the work itself.
- **Verify.** Same frames side by side: the named part is calmer and the accent still lands. Nothing
  outside the note changed.
- **Goes wrong when** the accent went quiet too, or the whole frame was darkened instead of the parts
  around the accent.

## bolder

"More punch", "it's flat", "make it hit". More conviction on one beat that already exists.

- **Assess.** Find the beat: hook, reveal, drop, payoff. Is it weak in itself, or because the moment
  before it is just as loud?
- **Decide.** Which beat (the hook when the note does not say; tell the person). One lever: quiet
  before it, a closer frame, a harder cut on the downbeat, or one sound.
- **Change.** Contrast first: make the moment before stiller. Then the beat: a closer frame, a larger
  scale change, one impact (`reveal` cue). One move at a time; two big moves cancel out. Bold is a
  decision, not an added effect.
- **Verify.** Watch the beat with the 2 s before it. It hits, and nothing else got louder.
- **Goes wrong when** everything got louder, effects pile up (shake, flash and zoom at once), or the
  bold moment outshouts the product or the ask.

## delight

"Fine but forgettable", "add something fun". One small detail that rewards attention, in the
brand's own voice.

- **Assess.** Is the piece right first? Delight on a piece that does not land is noise. Find a
  moment with room: a hold, a transition, the end card.
- **Decide.** Where (not the opening, which must be about the point) and whose voice (the brand's
  or the subject's, never a generic flourish).
- **Change.** One detail, two at most: the logo doing one thing it only does here, a sound on one
  hit, a line of copy a little warmer than it has to be. Small and quick.
- **Verify.** Seen fresh, it is noticed second, not first. Take it out: if nothing is lost, drop it.
- **Goes wrong when** it upstages the message, it is generic (confetti, sparkles, a bounce any piece
  could have), or it repeats in every scene.
