# Move: remix

How a review note maps to a named move: [`../../magnifito-video/reference/moves.md`](../../magnifito-video/reference/moves.md).

## remix

"The music's too loud", "can't hear her", "needs a sound there", "the mix", "it sounds cheap". The
sound: voice, music, effects and the master. A "quieter" note about sound lands here too.

- **Assess.** Measure each source's integrated loudness ([`mix-levels.md`](mix-levels.md)). Find
  what fills space instead of marking moments: a bed at one level under everything, a whoosh on
  every entrance. With music, compare cuts and hits with the beat grid
  ([`beat-and-sound-sync.md`](beat-and-sound-sync.md)).
- **Decide.** What leads: the voice or the music. Everything else sits under it. Which few
  moments get a sound.
- **Change.** Voice first; duck music and effects under it with the numbers in
  [`mix-levels.md`](mix-levels.md). Remove layers before lowering levels. Keep a hit on the cut that
  matters and silence before the drop; drop sounds that only fill. Master to the floor's loudness and
  true peak ([`../../magnifito-deliver/reference/floor.md`](../../magnifito-deliver/reference/floor.md)).
- **Verify.** `mgvideo inspect <final>` and `mgvideo validate` for loudness, true peak and dead
  stops; the momentary-loudness check in `beat-and-sound-sync.md` section 5 for jumps. A person
  listens once on phone speakers and once on headphones. Never say it sounds good.
- **Goes wrong when** the voice is clear on headphones and buried on a phone speaker, the bed pumps
  as it ducks (slower release or lower bed), or a true peak goes over because the meter was read by
  eye.
