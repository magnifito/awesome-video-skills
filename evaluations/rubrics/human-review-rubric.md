# Magnifito Super Video - Human Review Rubric

As specified in the implementation blueprint (Section 9 & 12), automated checks support the reviewer but do not replace human judgment. Every candidate release or client deliverable is scored across five core dimensions on a 1 to 5 scale:

| Dimension | 1 - Unacceptable | 3 - Competent / Acceptable | 5 - Exceptional |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Storytelling & Clarity** | Unclear core message; confusing sequence of claims; buried call-to-action. | Clear message; logical sequence; viewer immediately grasps the primary benefit. | Compelling hook within 3 seconds; memorable pacing; irresistible CTA. |
| **Typography & Readability** | Hard to read; awkward Bulgarian word wrap; insufficient contrast; text cut off. | Readable type scale; proper contrast (WCAG AA); verified Cyrillic glyphs. | Flawless typography hierarchy; natural kinetic reveal timing matching speech rate. |
| **Pacing & Motion Flow** | Jerky transitions; jarring speed changes; elements move without purpose. | Smooth easing curves; steady camera drift; stable layout rhythm. | Choreographed motion hierarchy; rhythmic motion cues guiding viewer focus effortlessly. |
| **Brand Fit & Restraint** | Generic template feel; unauthorized neon or glass; brand colors diluted. | Strict adherence to brand tokens; correct logos; prohibited treatments avoided. | Bespoke identity expression; elevated brand prestige; tailored design vocabulary. |
| **Sound & Mix Balance** | Speech unintelligible; loud music clipping; missing sync with visual events. | Intelligible voiceover; music ducked under dialogue; -16 LUFS loudness verified. | Broadcast-grade audio; immersive sound design; crisp transients and seamless ducking. |

## Production Gate Policy
- A deliverable requires a minimum score of **3.5 / 5.0** overall with no dimension below **3.0** to receive sign-off in `review-record.json`.
